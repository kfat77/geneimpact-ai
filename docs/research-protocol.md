# Research protocol

## Primary research question

For a pre-specified phenotype, which single-gene signals and gene-gene combinations show reproducible, calibrated associations after accounting for population structure, covariates, and multiple testing?

## Unit of analysis

- Genome build and variant annotation version must be recorded for every run.
- The phenotype definition, cohort inclusion/exclusion criteria, and outcome window are pre-registered.
- Variant-level quality control precedes gene-level aggregation.

## Modelling sequence

1. Train a simple, interpretable single-gene baseline.
2. Generate candidate interactions only from pre-declared biological networks or replicated evidence.
3. Use held-out cohorts for selection and independent cohorts for final replication.
4. Correct interaction discovery for the full search space and report calibration.
5. Publish effect estimates, uncertainty, cohort characteristics, and negative results.

## Minimum acceptance criteria

A finding is not promoted beyond exploratory status without an independent replication, uncertainty interval, sensitivity analysis, and documented ancestry-stratified performance.
